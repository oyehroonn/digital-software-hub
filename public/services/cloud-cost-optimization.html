What this is & who it's for

A cost engagement starts with a full billing audit: which resources are over-provisioned relative to actual usage, which reserved-capacity commitments make sense given real traffic patterns, and which resources (old snapshots, unattached volumes, idle load balancers) are billing with zero business value. We implement the fixes and set up cost-anomaly alerting so the same drift doesn't recur. Built for: any team whose cloud bill has grown faster than their user base, which is most teams a year or two post-launch.
