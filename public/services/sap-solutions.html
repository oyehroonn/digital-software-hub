What this is & who it's for

Most SAP pain isn't the core ERP functionality — it's the isolation. We build API integration layers between SAP and your CRM, e-commerce platform, or custom systems, extract SAP data into a modern BI/analytics layer, and support incremental modernization of SAP-adjacent processes without requiring a full re-implementation.
