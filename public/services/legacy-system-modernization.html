What this is & who it's for

Legacy modernization is scoped as a series of low-risk, independently shippable steps, not a big-bang rewrite: we map the existing system (often undocumented, sometimes with the original developers gone), identify the modules causing the most pain, and replace them one at a time behind a stable interface while the old system keeps running. Built for: teams running a critical platform on an aging stack no one fully understands anymore, where the business risk of touching it feels higher than the risk of leaving it alone.
