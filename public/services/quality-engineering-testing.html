What this is & who it's for

A dedicated QA layer covering three disciplines: functional testing (does the feature behave correctly), automation testing (is that verified on every commit, not once before launch), and performance testing (does it hold under real load, not just a demo's worth of traffic). We run this on our own delivery pipeline for every DSM build, and stand it up as a standalone practice for teams that already have a codebase and just need the discipline.

Built for: engineering teams shipping without a regression suite, products about to scale past their current load profile, and anyone who's had a "it worked in staging" production incident.

How this differs from a normal agency

AI-generated test coverage, human-reviewed gates.

A normal QA engagement writes test cases by hand against a spec, which means coverage is only as complete as the tester's patience. We use AI to generate exhaustive test-case coverage from specs and existing code paths, then have engineers review and harden the gates that actually block a release — wider coverage, in less time, without turning QA into a rubber stamp.
