What this is & who it's for

We audit candidate workflows for automation readiness: is the underlying process well-defined enough to automate, is the data clean enough to trust, and what's the actual cost of an AI mistake in this specific workflow versus a human one. The output is a ranked list of automation candidates with realistic ROI estimates, not a generic 'AI transformation roadmap' template. Built for: leadership teams under pressure to 'do something with AI' who want an honest assessment before committing budget.
