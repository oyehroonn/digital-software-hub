What this is & who it's for

Cluster design covers node pools sized for your actual workload mix, namespace and resource-quota strategy for multi-team clusters, ingress and service-mesh setup, and autoscaling policy (horizontal pod autoscaling, cluster autoscaling). We also operate clusters post-launch — upgrades, security patching, and incident response — since an unmaintained Kubernetes cluster is worse than no Kubernetes at all. Built for: teams running 5+ services that need independent scaling, and teams whose current container orchestration is a set of cron jobs and hope.
