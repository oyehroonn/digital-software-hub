What this is & who it's for

Hybrid architecture starts with classifying workloads by constraint: regulated data that must stay in-region or on-prem, latency-sensitive systems (factory floor, point-of-sale) that can't tolerate a round trip to the cloud, and everything else that benefits from public-cloud elasticity. We then design the private connectivity (VPN, dedicated interconnect) and the deployment pipeline that treats both environments as one system. Built for: regulated industries (finance, healthcare, government) and organizations with existing on-prem investment they're not ready to fully retire.
