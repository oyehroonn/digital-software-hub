What this is & who it's for

Platform engineering is the layer between "code is written" and "code is running reliably in production" — release pipelines, container orchestration, infrastructure defined as code instead of console clicks, and the monitoring that tells you something broke before a customer does. We build this as a standing capability inside your organization, not a one-off pipeline that nobody maintains after we leave.

Built for: engineering teams still deploying manually or on a fragile CI script, teams running Kubernetes without anyone dedicated to it, and organizations that need an SRE practice but aren't yet large enough to hire one in-house.
